Who we are

About Davidoff Coffee Ground Co.

It started in 1994 with a secondhand drum roaster, a bicycle, and one stubborn belief: coffee should be ground hours before you brew it, not months. Today we roast for cafés and kitchens across the country — but every bag still gets the small-batch treatment.

We buy directly from eighteen family farms, pay above fair-trade rates, and print the roast date on every single pouch.

  • Ground to order for your exact brewer
  • Roast date printed on every bag
  • Direct trade with 18 origin farms
