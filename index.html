The house

Where the bar meets the bean

The Smirnoff Vodka Coffee House began with one late-night question: why should the espresso martini live only after dinner? We built a house where coffee is treated like a spirit — measured, flavoured, and poured with intent.

Every drink is built on slow-extracted cold brew, infused with our flavour library, and finished at the intensity you choose — from °1 whisper to °5 midnight.

  • Nine flavours, from vanilla velvet to citrus noir
  • Five intensities — you set the strength of the pour
  • Zero-proof pours with all the theatre, none of the vodka
